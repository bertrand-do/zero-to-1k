# The reply framework — rules and evidence

A reply's reach is borrowed from the post it sits under. The job is to find posts that will get big, early enough that your reply is near the top.

## Evidence 1 — Rob Hallam's first two days (264 external replies, views captured ~2.5 days later)

"Hit" = reply got 100+ views.

| Post's final views | Replies | Hit rate |
|---|---|---|
| under 2k | 148 | 1% |
| 2k–10k | 48 | 10% |
| 10k–100k | 41 | 24% |
| 100k+ | 27 | 37% |

| Post age when he replied | Replies | Hit rate |
|---|---|---|
| under 15 min | 79 | 4% |
| 15–60 min | 28 | 21% |
| 1–3 h | 37 | 14% |
| 3–12 h | 53 | 13% |
| 12 h+ | 67 | 9% |

| Author followers (replies to original posts) | Replies | Hit rate |
|---|---|---|
| under 1k | 38 | 3% |
| 1k–3k | 39 | 10% |
| 3k–10k | 29 | 21% |
| 10k–100k | 27 | 26% |
| 100k+ | 22 | 9% |

- 20 of 27 hits were under posts that ended at 10k+ views; 20 of 27 were direct replies to the original post.
- Rob never replied under a post that ended below 2% of its author's follower count.
- Low replies-per-view on the post did NOT hurt (under 0.1% replies/view → 41% hits): fewer competitors.
- Day 1 topics: ~50% model/coding takes, ~34% jokes/random viral, 9% founder posts. F4F "let's be X friends" only started on day 2, after his intro post.

## Evidence 2 — velocity tracker (90 fresh AI posts, checked every 30 min for ~8 h, 25 Sep 2026)

| Views at ~30 min | n | Reached 10k |
|---|---|---|
| under 300 | 41 | 7% |
| 300–1k | 16 | 44% |
| 1k–3k | 9 | 33% |
| 3k–10k | 2 | 100% |

| Views at ~60 min | n | Reached 10k |
|---|---|---|
| under 300 | 43 | 7% |
| 300–1k | 17 | 0% |
| 1k–3k | 20 | 40% |
| 3k–10k | 7 | 71% |
| 10k+ | 3 | 100% |

Motion (likes ≥1% of views or 3+ replies) at 15–60 min: 14/29 reached 10k vs 2/8 without. Weak but positive; kept.

## Evidence 3 — Bertrand's day 1 (245 replies, 25 Sep 2026)

| | Replies | Typical views | 100+ views |
|---|---|---|---|
| Before finder (SuperX keyword feed) | 115 | 13 | 11 |
| With finder | 130 | 39 | 35 |

- Replies under posts <2k views averaged 5 views; 2k–10k → 11; 10k+ → 30.
- Best topic per reply: Muse/Meta (avg 99 views). Model news in volume. Founder/indie posts: ~2 views (tiny posts).
- Replies with media (GIF/image/video) were the top 3 (3,051 / 2,084 / 1,189 views). Generic reactions ("banger", "context?", emoji) got 1–2 views.

## Rules in the finder (rising tab)

| Rule | Why |
|---|---|
| Post < 60 min old (owner call; Rob's best window 15–60 min) | Evidence 1 |
| GO if ≥1k views at 15–60 min; WATCH if <15 min with ≥300 | Evidence 2 (300+ at 30 min already strong) |
| Author ≥1k followers | Evidence 1: 3% hits under 1k |
| Skip 20k+ accounts where views < 1% of followers (post flopping) | Evidence 1 (never <2% final) |
| Motion: likes ≥1% of views or 3+ replies (after 15 min) | Evidence 2 (weak) |
| Author's last 5 posts: median ≥3 replies | dead-engagement accounts (views, no conversation) |
| Like-pod: 20+ likes and 0 replies after 15 min → skip | observed pattern (engagement farming) |
| Crypto names/handles, $TICKERs, trading talk, politics → skip | owner rules |
| Rank by views per minute | fastest-rising first |

## Rules in the connect tab

Top 8 connect/say-hi/what-are-you-building threads (≥10 replies, not milestone/celebration posts) → commenters from the last 60 min with 100–20k followers, account ≥60 days old, a builder bio, no crypto/politics/bots, a real comment (≥4 words, not a congratulation).
