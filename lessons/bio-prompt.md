Bio Prompt for Agent

You are helping me write my X bio.

The idea: my bio is not a résumé. It's the title of a story people can follow. A good story needs two things:
- A plot: a number that will change, so people come back to check if it moved.
- A character: something real about me, so people care. Flaws included. Too "perfect" feels fake and people drift away.

The goal is profile conversion: someone lands on my profile, reads the bio in 2 seconds, and wants to follow the next chapter.

Step 1: Check what you know
Use what you know about me: my past, what I'm building, my goals, my struggles, my numbers.
If you're missing any of these, ask me up to 5 short questions first. Never invent facts, numbers or struggles.

Step 2: Write 5 bios
Formula: [who I was / what happened] + [what I'm building now] + [a number that will change]
Past, present, future. Character, then plot.

Rules:
- Max 160 characters. Count carefully. Emojis count as 2.
- 2 short (under 120), 2 medium (120 to 150), 1 using almost all 160.
- Every bio needs a live number or public goal people can check later: $0 → $10k MRR, month number, progress bar, follower goal.
- Every bio needs one true human detail: a job I left, a product I sold, years I lost, a health problem, a family constraint, a struggle I'm living through.
- Vulnerability is a plus if it's true. Don't force trauma. If there's no painful story, use a real constraint: time, money, starting from zero.
- Write like a person, not a brand. No "passionate about", "helping founders scale" or guru language.
- Don't copy the examples. Steal the structure, keep my personality.
- Show the exact character count after each bio.

Examples of the structure (don't copy):
- ex-[company]. Building [product] in public. 🟩⬜⬜⬜⬜⬜⬜⬜ $0 → $10k MRR
- Dad. 60 min a day. One-person company. Building [product]. Month 3 • $0 → $10k MRR
- Sold software. Lost years to burnout. Rebuilding [product] now. Sharing every number.
- Family first. Shipping after the kids sleep. [product] • 🟩⬜⬜⬜⬜⬜⬜⬜ $800 / $10k
- Stuck at 400 followers for 2 years. Starting over properly. [product] • goal: 1k followers + first $1k MRR

Step 3: After the 5 bios, add
1. One sentence per bio: what's the plot, what's the character.
2. The one you recommend for my account size, and why.
3. A 4 to 6 line pinned post that tells the same story and ends with an invitation to say hi.
