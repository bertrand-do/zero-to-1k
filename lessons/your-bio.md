# Your bio

Your bio is not a résumé.

It's the title of a story people can follow.

People don't follow a clever one-liner. They follow a plot they can check back on:

- Is he still going?
- Did the number move?
- What happens next?

## Why your bio matters

Most people skim over their bio.

But a bio that doesn't feel like a real, relatable person will kill your growth.

Why? Because your bio decides your profile conversion rate: the % of people who visit your profile and then follow you.

Here is mine during my first 3 days of growth:

- Day 1: 226 / 444 = 50.9%
- Day 2: 213 / 603 = 35.3%
- Day 3: 92 / 299 = 30.8%

Average: 39.4%.

Most accounts convert far lower. And when a profile converts badly, there is usually one reason. A bad bio.

## A good story needs two things

A plot: something that moves, so people come back.
A character: someone real, so people care.

Your bio gives them both in one glance.

## The plot: a number that will change

Numbers turn your account into a show. Anyone understands them in two seconds:

- Goal: $0 → $10k MRR
- 🟩⬜⬜⬜⬜⬜⬜⬜ $1.2k / $10k
- Month 4 • $840 MRR

Then update the number in your bio and post about it.

- Bio = the title of the show.
- Posts = the next episode.

## The character: your real life

Don't invent a founder persona.

A polished bio reads like marketing. A specific one reads like a person.

Use what actually happened:

- A job you left.
- A product you sold.
- Years you lost.
- A health problem.
- Being a parent with 60 minutes a day.

Everyone pretends to be perfect. The truth is that we all struggle, and people see themselves in that.

No painful story? Don't force one. Use a real constraint instead: time, money, starting from zero.

## The formula

[who you were] + [what you're building now] + [a number that will change]

Past, present, future. Character, then plot.

## Why mine works

ex-Google Maps. Sold $1.8M of software.
Lost 6 years to depression and sleep apnea.
Rebuilding now with Dezygn + an AI photo agency.
Sharing all the numbers.

- **Proof:** Google Maps, $1.8M sold. Why you should listen.
- **Wound:** Six lost years. Why you should care.
- **Rebuild:** What I'm doing now. What you'll watch.
- **Promise:** Sharing all the numbers. The plot keeps updating.
